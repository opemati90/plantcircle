# 00 — Product brief

## One line
Find your people, cook together, buy food together — near you or online, anywhere in the world.

## Problem
Food communities organise in WhatsApp groups, spreadsheets and payment links. Hosts lose hours to head-counting, “who brings what”, waitlists and reminders; newcomers can’t find anything; nothing connects people across cities.

## Who it’s for
- **Members:** people who want to eat more plants, meet people and learn to cook — from curious beginners to long-time vegans. Any diet is welcome; diet is optional and private.
- **Hosts:** home cooks, cooking teachers and organisers who run cooking nights, potlucks, classes or group orders.
- **Stores (later):** shops, farms and online suppliers that sell plant-based food and want group orders.
- **Staff:** a small trust & safety team.

## Core loop
Discover a Plan → Join (sign up only at this moment) → Attend and get checked in → Feedback, connect, join the Circle → Host your own Plan → Share the link → New members arrive.

## What makes it different
1. **Built for hosts:** AI draft, one-question-at-a-time creation, waitlists, “what to bring”, check-in, recipe card after.
2. **Link-first growth:** anyone can RSVP from a shared link in the browser without installing the app.
3. **Global layer:** live online cook-alongs and *global editions* (“one recipe, many tables”) so nobody lands in an empty city.
4. **Group orders:** neighbours pool bulk orders of staples; pay the organizer at pickup.
5. **Impact you can trust:** estimated footprint per Plan, packaging and food saved — labelled as estimates with a published method.
6. **Safety and accessibility designed in.**

## Success metric (North Star)
**Meaningful Community Participations:** verified attendance (host check-in), verified online attendance, or a completed group order. An RSVP alone never counts.

## Business model (phased)
Free core forever. Later: 8% service fee on paid workshop tickets (paid by guests), Host Pro €9.99/month (4% guest fee, AI, recurring Plans, memberships, co-hosts, insights), store Partner listings, and eventually a fee on completed store orders.

## Glossary
- **Plan** — any gathering: cooking night, potluck, class, meetup, group order, online class, global edition table.
- **Circle** — a community group (local or worldwide; open, by request, or invite-only).
- **Global edition** — a recipe + live intro published by one host that other hosts adopt as local in-person tables, linked by a 15-minute live call.
- **Group order** — non-binding interest in a bulk order; paid to the organizer at pickup.
- **Check-in** — host confirms a guest attended; the only thing that counts as participation.
- **Seeds** (later) — in-app reward credits for hosts; no cash-out.
